GLOBAL AFFAIRS V3 — GIFT CARD AND ELIMINATION DIPLOMACY FIX

Upload every file in this folder as one complete mod update.

Changes:
1. Gift Cards are blocked when the giver is at war and the recipient is not participating in the same active war(s).
2. The blocked order produces an in-game explanation naming the recipient and missing war opponent.
3. Players and AI with no territories, or who surrendered, are marked eliminated.
4. Wars involving an eliminated nation end automatically and disappear from Current Wars.
5. Pending war declarations, peace/NAP/alliance offers, active alliances/NAPs, war events, and faction membership involving the eliminated nation are cleared.
6. Diplomacy history keeps a record that the war ended because of elimination.

Live test after upload:
- A at war with B; A gifts to C while C is at peace with B: blocked.
- Put C at war with B; repeat A to C gift: allowed.
- Eliminate or surrender B; advance the turn: A vs B no longer appears in Current Wars.
