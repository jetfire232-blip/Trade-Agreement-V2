Trade Agreement V2 is a Commerce-focused economic and diplomacy mod that adds trade agreements, investments, taxation, ideology, stock markets, flagship companies, ETFs, dividends, and enhanced AI behavior.

TRADE AGREEMENTS

Players may propose bilateral trade agreements with other human or AI nations.

Active trade agreements provide both nations with additional Commerce income each turn. Trade benefits are based on each nation's current Commerce income and the host-configured trade settings.

Players can view active agreements, find potential partners, and review recent diplomacy activity from the mod menu.


INVESTMENTS

Players and AI nations may create and invest in economic projects.

Projects have:
- Funding goals
- Investment limits
- Development times
- Risk levels
- Potential returns

Successful projects return invested principal plus profit.

Failed projects return the configured recovery amount.

Projects that fail to reach their funding goal before the deadline refund committed principal.


STOCK MARKET

Eligible nations may establish a flagship company.

Each company has:
- A stock price
- Total shares
- Publicly available shares
- Market capitalization
- Company strategy
- Shareholders
- Price history

Players and AI nations may buy and sell shares.

Company prices react to market activity and economic conditions.


COMPANY STRATEGIES

Flagship companies may follow one of several strategies:

Growth
Focuses more heavily on expansion and provides lower dividend payouts.

Balanced
Balances company growth and shareholder returns.

Dividend
Provides larger dividend payouts to shareholders.


DIVIDENDS

Companies may distribute dividends to shareholders each turn.

Dividend payments are calculated per share.

Fractional dividend amounts are carried forward until they accumulate into whole Commerce gold.

Players can track total dividends received through their portfolio.


SHARE ISSUANCE

Flagship company owners may issue additional public shares.

Available issuance options are:
- 10 shares
- 25 shares
- 50 shares

Issuing additional shares increases the company's total share count and places new shares on the public market.

Existing shareholders are diluted because their existing shares represent a smaller percentage of the company.

Share issuance has a 5-turn cooldown.


STOCK SPLITS

Companies with very high stock prices may perform a 2-for-1 stock split.

During a split:
- Stock price is approximately halved
- Total shares double
- Existing shareholder share counts double

The economic value of each shareholder's position remains approximately the same.


ETF

The market includes an ETF representing leading companies.

The ETF periodically rebalances based on market performance.

Players may:
- Buy ETF shares
- Sell ETF shares
- Receive ETF dividends
- View ETF price history and portfolio value


TAXATION

Each nation may choose a tax policy.

Available policies include:

Low Tax
Encourages markets and investment but produces less direct Commerce.

Standard Tax
Provides balanced economic effects.

High Tax
Provides more direct Commerce but creates larger penalties to market and investment activity.


IDEOLOGY

Each nation may select an economic ideology.

Available ideologies include:

- Free Market
- Capitalist
- Social Democratic
- State Capitalist
- Socialist

Ideology affects Commerce, market performance, investment behavior, company confidence, and AI economic decisions.


DIPLOMACY

The Diplomacy section allows players to:

- Search for other nations
- View player economic information
- View ideology and tax policy
- Review diplomatic relationships
- View NAP and alliance status
- View flagship company information
- Review recent diplomacy history


SMART AI

AI nations use enhanced economic and military decision-making.

AI nations may:
- Buy and sell stocks
- Invest in projects
- Build cities
- Maintain strategic gold reserves
- Adjust spending based on economic conditions
- Evaluate territory defense
- Avoid unnecessary troop stacking on peaceful borders
- Save Commerce for future wars
- Reinforce active war fronts more aggressively
- Adjust decisions based on AI economic profiles and ideology

During peace, AI nations generally maintain moderate border defenses while preserving part of their Commerce.

When war begins, more of the accumulated reserve becomes available for military spending.


MOD MENU

Game > Mod > Trade Agreement V2

The menu includes:

- Overview
- Diplomacy
- Trade Agreements
- Investments
- Markets
- Taxation
- Global Economy
- How It Works
- Customize Tabs


HOST CONFIGURATION

The host may configure many economic and gameplay settings before the game begins.

Settings can affect trade, investments, taxation, market behavior, AI behavior, and other economic systems.


GENERAL NOTES

This mod is designed around Commerce games.

Economic systems continue to develop over multiple turns, so market prices, company ownership, investments, dividends, taxation, diplomacy, and AI strategy may change significantly throughout a game.

For best results, players should regularly review the mod menu and monitor their nation's economy, investments, diplomatic relationships, and market positions.


FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

United Nations features are planned for a future V3 release.

Some temporary UN-related configuration may still exist in the current codebase, but the UN system is not active or supported in Trade Agreement V2.


STRATEGIC RESOURCES (V3)

When enabled by the host, nations receive territory-based strategic resource deposits using 2026 slot profiles. Resources produce every turn, transfer with territory ownership, may be traded through recurring contracts, and shortages reduce usable Commerce / military spending power. Players can invest gold to upgrade facilities on deposits they control.

STRATEGIC RESOURCES

When enabled by the host, territories may contain strategic Resource Hubs. Oil, Gas, Uranium, Iron, Food, Rare Earths, and optional advanced resources influence economic output, military readiness, mobilization, and national unrest. Existing armies are never removed by resource shortages. Resource Hubs use one shared map icon with a number showing the territory's combined facility level.

UNITED NATIONS / SECURITY COUNCIL

When enabled by the host, the United Nations creates a Security Council with configurable permanent and rotating seats. Permanent members have veto power. Initial resolution types include sanctions, embargoes, aid, condemnations, and ceasefires. AI council members vote automatically while human council members vote through the mod menu.
