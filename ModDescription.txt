GLOBAL AFFAIRS V3

Global Affairs V3 is a Commerce-focused economy, diplomacy, market, strategic-resource, United Nations, and enhanced-AI mod for War.app.

CORE ECONOMY

Players manage Commerce through taxation, ideology, trade agreements, investments, flagship companies, stocks, dividends, ETF holdings, and strategic resources. Commerce is both economic power and an important source of military purchasing power.

TRADE AGREEMENTS

Players may propose bilateral Trade Agreements with human or AI nations. Active agreements provide recurring Commerce benefits while the nations remain eligible to trade. War, diplomatic restrictions, and some UN actions can interrupt or end trade.

INVESTMENTS

Players and AI may create and fund investment projects. Projects have funding goals, development times, risk, potential returns, and failure/recovery rules. Successful projects return principal plus profit; unfunded projects refund committed principal under the configured rules.

MARKETS

Eligible nations may establish flagship companies. Players and AI can buy and sell shares, receive dividends, track cost basis and profit/loss, trade an ETF, and view market history. Companies support Growth, Balanced, and Dividend strategies. Owners may issue new public shares with a cooldown, and high-priced companies may perform 2-for-1 stock splits.

TAXATION AND IDEOLOGY

Tax policies: Low, Standard, High.

Ideologies include Free Market, Capitalist, Social Democratic, State Capitalist, Socialist, Communist, Fascist, and Nationalist. Ideology influences Commerce, market/investment behavior, company confidence, and AI economic decisions.

DIPLOMACY AND WAR

Diplomacy includes player search/overview, wars, peace, Non-Aggression Pacts, alliances, relationship history, and Current Wars. Current Wars tracks war duration, attacks, combat losses, territories captured, and direct wartime decision costs.

War Events can present human players with strategic choices. Players have one turn to answer; an unanswered event automatically receives a balanced response. Existing armies are never deleted by these systems.

AI MANAGER

When allowed by the host, human players may enable an AI Manager with a defined per-turn budget. The manager handles markets and investments only. It does not control diplomacy, trade agreements, tax policy, ideology, or military orders. Canceling it takes effect after the configured one-turn delay.

STRATEGIC RESOURCES

When enabled, territories may contain Oil, Gas, Uranium, Iron, Food, Rare Earths, and optional Coal, Copper, and Lithium. Production follows territory ownership, so capturing a resource territory transfers its production after the turn resolves.

Nations have resource requirements. Falling below them can reduce Commerce, lower Military Readiness, and increase unrest. Existing armies are not removed by shortages. Players may develop/upgrade resource facilities with gold and create recurring resource trade contracts.

Each resource territory shows one dominant-resource icon with a numeric badge representing its combined facility/deposit level. The host may disable resource map icons globally while leaving the resource economy active.

UNITED NATIONS / SECURITY COUNCIL

The UN is optional and host-controlled. The Security Council has configurable permanent and rotating seats. Permanent members have veto power over Security Council resolutions. Human council members vote YES / NO / ABSTAIN and AI council members vote automatically.

Initial resolutions:
- Economic Sanctions
- Trade Embargo
- Economic Aid
- Condemn Nation
- Peace / Ceasefire

Proposal cooldowns prevent resolution spam, and the UN screen explains the effect of each resolution before submission.

SMART AI

AI nations can manage strategic reserves, markets, investments, city construction, trade strategy, diplomacy, peaceful-border troop limits, and wartime behavior. Large games automatically stagger non-urgent AI work to reduce advance-turn time while critical maintenance continues every turn.

HOW IT WORKS

The in-game How It Works guide provides a Quick Start, examples, resource roles, Military Readiness explanations, map-icon guidance, market and investment instructions, diplomacy/war guidance, AI Manager instructions, UN consequences, and host configuration guidance.

HOST CONFIGURATION

Major systems including Resources, resource map icons, the United Nations, AI Manager availability, War Events, market/economic settings, and other mechanics are host-configurable.

PERFORMANCE

Global Affairs V3 includes large-game performance safeguards such as per-turn economic caches, shared AI threat calculation, cached territory lists, reduced repeated data writes, and automatic AI work staggering for larger and Mega Games.

GENERAL NOTES

This mod is designed primarily for Commerce games. Economic and diplomatic systems develop across multiple turns. For best results, use the in-game How It Works guide and review your nation's Commerce, resource position, Military Readiness, diplomacy, investments, and market exposure regularly.
